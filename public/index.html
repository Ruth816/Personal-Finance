Vehicles & equipment

Family cars (loan or fully paid, registration, insurance, service) and home equipment such as the solar panels (installed when, warranty, next maintenance). Each has a records log where you can upload receipts, OR/CR, policies and service reports.

To save vehicles to your account, run supabase/vehicles.sql once in Supabase (SQL Editor → New query → paste → Run), then reload.

Life insurance policies

Premiums you pay still go in the Expenses log (Life insurance, Health insurance). This page keeps the policy details, what is paid up to, and when each one ends. Policy numbers show only their last 4 characters.

To save policies to your account, run supabase/insurance.sql once in Supabase (SQL Editor → New query → paste → Run), then reload.
